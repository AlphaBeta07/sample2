About AISA

The Artificial Intelligence & Student Association (AISA) is a student-led organization dedicated to promoting AI education, research, and innovation. We bring together students from diverse backgrounds who share a passion for artificial intelligence and its applications.

Our mission is to create a collaborative environment where students can learn, experiment, and grow their AI skills through workshops, projects, competitions, and networking events.
